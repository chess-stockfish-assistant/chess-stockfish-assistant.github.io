About the App

Chess Assistant is a chess and checkers study and analysis tool designed to help players learn, improve their skills, and better understand game positions.

Chess analysis is performed locally on your device using the open-source Stockfish chess engine. Checkers analysis is also performed locally on your device.

The App can read supported chess and checkers boards displayed on your screen, through Android's Accessibility Service or through screenshots, and can provide move suggestions, study hints, style-based analysis, and optional automation features such as Auto Play.

Analysis and suggestions are generated automatically and may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for a particular position. They are intended for study and entertainment and do not constitute professional advice of any kind.
