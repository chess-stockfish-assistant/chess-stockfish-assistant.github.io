Chess Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Chess Assistant is a chess study and analysis tool designed to help players learn, improve their skills, and better understand chess positions.

Chess analysis is performed locally on the user's device using the open-source Stockfish chess engine.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Chess Assistant accesses, collects, processes, and stores, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

Purpose

Chess Assistant is intended as an educational and training tool for chess players who want to:

  • Study and analyze chess positions
  • Learn from move classifications such as Brilliant, Great, Best, Inaccuracy, Mistake, and Blunder
  • Explore different play styles, including Aggressive, Defensive, and Positional
  • Improve their understanding of openings, middlegames, and endgames

Users are responsible for using Chess Assistant in accordance with the rules and terms of service of the chess platforms they use.

Chess Assistant is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Chess.com or Lichess.

Data Collection

We collect as little information as necessary to operate, protect, and improve Chess Assistant.

The information that may leave your device is limited to:

  • Free-tier usage information
  • Usage and diagnostic statistics
  • Optional Board-Reading Reports, only when you have agreed to send them

This information uses a pseudonymous device identifier as described below.

Chess Assistant does not require account registration.

Your chosen username is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Chess analysis using Stockfish is performed locally on your device.

Chess positions, moves, and game information are not normally transmitted to our servers. Limited chess-game information may be transmitted only through the optional Board-Reading Reports described below.

We do not collect advertising data and do not use your information to track you across other apps or websites.

Free-Tier Usage Counter

The free version of Chess Assistant allows a limited number of analyses per day.

To enforce this limit and prevent abuse, the free version sends the following information to a server operated by the developer through an encrypted HTTPS connection:

  • A one-way SHA-256 hash of the Android identifier assigned to the app on the device
  • A randomly generated installation identifier
  • The number of free analyses used
  • The installed app version

The Android identifier is hashed before being sent. The server does not receive the original identifier.

The hash is not associated by Chess Assistant with your name, email address, Google account, or Chess.com/Lichess username.

Google Play Integrity

The free version may use the Google Play Integrity API to verify that the app is genuine, was installed through an authorized Google Play environment, and is running in an environment that satisfies applicable integrity checks.

Google performs the integrity check under Google's own privacy practices.

Our server receives the integrity result and may store whether the verification succeeded. If verification fails, the server may also store a short technical reason code.

If you are using a free trial of Premium features, the server also stores the date on which the trial ends. A trial may be ended early when required integrity checks fail.

Retention

Free-tier counter records are automatically deleted 30 days after the app last reports activity.

After that period, the server may retain only the one-way device hash together with:

  • The date the identifier was first seen
  • The date a free trial ended, when applicable

This limited record is used to prevent reinstalling the app from repeatedly starting new free trials.

It is automatically deleted 400 days after the app last reports activity.

For abuse prevention, the server may temporarily process your IP address in memory for up to one hour. The IP address is not stored together with usage-counter records, usage statistics, or Board-Reading Reports.

Premium subscribers do not send the free-tier usage-counter information described in this section.

Usage and Diagnostic Statistics

To understand how Chess Assistant is used, improve reliability, identify technical problems, and prevent abuse, free and Premium versions may send usage and diagnostic statistics to the developer's server over HTTPS.

These statistics may include:

  • App events such as opening the app, starting or stopping the assistant, and navigating through relevant setup or feature screens
  • Accessibility, overlay, and notification permission prompts and the user's response to those prompts
  • Premium offer and free-trial screens displayed by the app
  • The number of analyses used during a trial
  • Premium upgrade and purchase attempts
  • Display of the Google Play rating card
  • Notifications indicating that free analyses are available again
  • Whether you accepted or declined optional Board-Reading Reports
  • Selected settings such as supported chess platform, play style, analysis depth, and enabled or disabled options
  • Whether Premium or a free trial is active
  • Session duration
  • Number of suggestions and analyses during a session
  • Whether a chess board was successfully detected
  • Whether a daily usage limit or pause was reached
  • Counts related to board-reading reliability, such as successfully read positions, incomplete boards, turn-detection fallbacks, move-list scrolling, color detection, and technical reasons why a board could not be read
  • App version
  • Android version
  • Device manufacturer and model
  • App language
  • Country
  • Security or installation status of the app

Country information may be determined from the SIM card, mobile network, or device language/region information. Chess Assistant does not request GPS or location permission for this purpose.

These usage statistics do not contain:

  • Chess board positions
  • Chess moves
  • Player usernames
  • Chat messages
  • Passwords
  • Content read from the Accessibility Service

Board-reading statistics contain counts, states, and technical reason codes rather than the contents of the chess screen.

Usage statistics are associated with the same pseudonymous one-way device hash and installation identifier described above.

They are used only to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Chess Assistant.

They are not sold or used for advertising.

Usage and diagnostic statistics are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Board-Reading Reports (Optional)

Chess Assistant includes an optional diagnostic reporting feature designed to help identify and fix problems with automatic chess-board reading.

Board-Reading Reports are sent only if you explicitly agree to this feature.

You can change your choice at any time in the assistant's settings.

When enabled, a report may be generated when:

  • Chess Assistant cannot correctly read a board displayed on screen
  • A supported chess app has remained on screen for approximately 30 seconds without a board being successfully detected
  • The detected side to move appears inconsistent
  • A detected move does not fit the reconstructed position
  • You manually select the Report option and choose a reason for the report

Reports are limited to information relevant to diagnosing board-reading problems.

A report may contain:

  • The position and dimensions of accessibility elements on the screen
  • The type of those elements, such as text or button
  • Technical names assigned by the chess app to screens or interface elements, such as board_view
  • Chess-related text such as moves, move numbers, piece and square descriptions, and chess clocks
  • The chess position reconstructed by Chess Assistant
  • When necessary for diagnosing an invalid move, the reconstructed position before that move
  • Which side Chess Assistant believes is to move
  • Which side Chess Assistant believes the user is playing
  • The detected board location
  • The most recent chess move suggested by Chess Assistant
  • The supported chess application and its version
  • Screen dimensions
  • App language
  • Chess Assistant version
  • The pseudonymous device hash and installation identifier described above

Before a report is transmitted, unrelated text is excluded on the device.

This includes information such as:

  • Player names
  • Ratings
  • Chat messages
  • Menu text
  • Other unrelated interface text

For excluded text, the report may retain only technical information such as the text length when needed for diagnosing interface structure.

Board-Reading Reports are used only to diagnose and improve board-reading functionality.

They are not sold or used for advertising.

Reports are automatically deleted after 14 days.

Chess Assistant sends no more than five automatically generated Board-Reading Reports per day.

Accessibility Service

Chess Assistant uses Android's Accessibility Service only when the user explicitly enables the automatic board-reading feature.

The Accessibility Service allows Chess Assistant to identify and reconstruct chess positions displayed in supported chess applications, including Chess.com and Lichess.

To provide this functionality, the Accessibility Service may read accessibility elements related to the chess game, including:

  • Chess piece positions
  • Board squares and descriptions
  • Moves and move numbers
  • Chess clocks
  • Information necessary to determine the reconstructed position
  • Information necessary to determine which side is to move
  • Information necessary to determine board orientation

This information is processed locally on the user's device to provide Chess Assistant's chess study and analysis features using the Stockfish engine.

The Accessibility Service is not used to intentionally access or collect:

  • Passwords
  • Private messages or conversations
  • Contacts
  • Payment information
  • Content from unrelated applications

Information obtained through the Accessibility Service is not normally transmitted to our servers.

The only exception is the optional Board-Reading Reports described above, which require the user's separate consent before they are enabled.

When Board-Reading Reports are enabled, limited chess-game and interface information necessary to diagnose board-reading problems may be transmitted as described in the “Board-Reading Reports” section.

When reading a game displayed by Lichess, Chess Assistant may temporarily scroll the visible move list in order to read earlier chess moves and then return the move list to its previous/latest position.

This interaction is performed only as part of automatic board reconstruction.

Users can disable the Accessibility Service at any time through Android settings.

Disabling the service prevents Chess Assistant from automatically reading boards from supported chess applications. Features that do not require automatic board reading can continue to be used.

In-App Purchases

Premium features are available through Google Play In-App Purchases.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

Chess Assistant and its developer do not receive or store your payment-card or other payment information.

Google Play may provide the app with information necessary to determine purchase and subscription status.

Data Stored on Your Device

Chess Assistant stores certain information locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences or equivalent Android storage mechanisms.

This may include:

  • Your chosen username, used only for display purposes
  • App settings and preferences
  • Analysis depth
  • Selected play style
  • Feature preferences
  • Premium subscription status
  • Free-tier usage information

Except for information specifically described in the Free-Tier Usage Counter, Usage and Diagnostic Statistics, and optional Board-Reading Reports sections, this locally stored information is not transmitted to our servers.

Local app information is removed according to Android's app-data and uninstall behavior when the application is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Data Security

Information transmitted between Chess Assistant and the developer's server is sent using encrypted HTTPS connections.

We limit server-side collection to information necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the app.

Retention periods are limited according to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Chess Assistant.

Used to process Premium purchases and subscriptions.

Google Play Integrity API

Used by the free version to help verify the integrity and installation environment of the app and to prevent abuse of free usage and trial features.

The server used for Chess Assistant's free-tier counters, usage statistics, and optional Board-Reading Reports is operated by the developer and is not considered a separate third-party analytics or advertising service.

Chess Assistant does not use third-party advertising services for the data processing described in this Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Chess Assistant is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that information from a child under 13 has been collected contrary to this policy or applicable requirements, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

User Choices

Depending on the feature, users can:

  • Choose whether to enable Android's Accessibility Service for automatic board reading
  • Choose whether to enable optional Board-Reading Reports
  • Disable Board-Reading Reports later through Chess Assistant settings
  • Disable the Accessibility Service through Android settings
  • Clear locally stored app data through Android settings
  • Uninstall Chess Assistant at any time

Some features cannot function without the permissions or processing necessary to provide them.
