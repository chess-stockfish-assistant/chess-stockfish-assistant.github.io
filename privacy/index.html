Chess Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Overview

Chess Assistant is a chess and checkers study and analysis app for Android, listed on Google Play as Chess Stockfish Assistant.

Analysis is performed locally on your device. The app does not require an account, and any username you choose stays on your device.

This policy explains what information may leave your device, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect as little information as necessary to operate, protect, and improve the app. Information that may leave your device is limited to:

  • Free-tier usage information, such as a one-way hash of the app's Android identifier, an installation identifier, the number of analyses used, and the app version
  • Usage and diagnostic statistics, such as app events, screens and options used, permission responses, purchase and subscription status, session information, app version, Android version, device model, language, and country
  • Whether supported chess or checkers apps are installed, to help decide which apps to support
  • Technical diagnostic reports, and a small image of the board area only when you enable this option, when a board cannot be read
  • Purchase verification information, including the Google Play purchase token, when you buy or restore Premium

These records use a pseudonymous device identifier and are not linked to your name, email address, or Google account.

We do not collect advertising data and do not track you across other apps or websites.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide free features and trials, verify purchases, prevent abuse, diagnose technical problems, and improve the app.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Permissions

Some features require Android permissions. You choose whether to enable them and can turn them off at any time in Android settings. Disabling a permission may prevent related features from working.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information for advertising. Payments and purchase verification are handled through Google Play, and Google processes information under its own privacy practices. Our server is operated by the developer.

Retention

  • Usage and diagnostic statistics: up to 90 days
  • Diagnostic reports: up to 14 days
  • Board images: up to 30 days
  • Free-tier counter records: 30 days after the last activity
  • A limited anti-abuse record, such as a one-way device hash and dates, for up to 400 days
  • Daily backups of the counter file: up to 7 days

Security

Information sent to our server uses encrypted HTTPS connections. We limit collection and retention to what is necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can disable permissions, turn off board images, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or request deletion of the data collected from your device.

Data Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to joarlemafra@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", your device model, and the approximate dates you used the app. We will delete the data and confirm by email.

The limited anti-abuse record described above may be kept for up to 400 days after the app last reports activity, to prevent repeated free trials.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When it is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will also be updated.
